StudyBuddy Plan

1. Site Name

The site will be named StudyBuddy. It came to me when my cousin was complaining about how everyone is just studying on their own for their exam but needed someone to study with. So I came with an idea to gather all the required info on the candidates is his year, then it came to me that what if everyone else needs someone to study with, maybe a friend. That is how the name came to be StudyBuddy.

2. Purpose

StudyBuddy will be helping students find relevant information on other students so that they can find their suitable study buddy. It can also help parents see which subjects their children have registered for. The top students also get some recognition which may boost their will to continue topping the charts.

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1:

"I wonder which subjects my son registered for. Where can i see that information"

Scenario 2:

"I can not figure out this equation let my find my teacher's contact number."

Scenario 3:

"This topic is very complex for but my teaccher is busy. Let me find the top student in biology"
